Your phone's signal icon reports radio strength — how loudly the nearest mast or router is shouting. It says nothing about whether data is moving. Everyone has stood somewhere with five bars and a page that will not load.

Honest Signal measures the connection instead. While open, it sends timed latency probes and periodically downloads a real 120 KB transfer sample. In the background, it uses a lighter probe between transfer samples. It blends measured latency, loss and throughput into a quality score from 0 to 5 bars — on Wi-Fi, 4G or 5G — with a plain-English verdict.

A LIVE SCORE IN YOUR STATUS BAR

The score lives in your status bar as a small indicator icon, next to the one your phone already draws. With the indicator on, background measurements are attempted every 2 seconds when the service can run; slow networks and Android power management can delay updates. Free, not a Pro feature.

The indicator is one silent, ongoing notification. It returns after a restart.

WHAT IT MEASURES

• Latency — timed round trips to public connectivity endpoints
• Jitter and loss — measured across foreground probes
• Throughput — timed from a real download, not from ping
• Wi-Fi or mobile data, beside the score

THE CASE A PING-ONLY TOOL MISSES

A link can answer pings promptly while no data moves through it. Latency and jitter look excellent then, so a ping-only tool calls it healthy. Honest Signal takes a real transfer sample: when probes answer but the download will not complete, it caps the score at 2 bars and says why — the "full bars, no data" failure pinging cannot see.

NO MYSTERY SCORE

"How the score works" is a screen in the app, not a footnote: every curve, weight, threshold and cap that produces the number is written down.

CONTROL YOUR DATA USE

A single background latency probe costs about 700 B, or roughly 30 MB over a full day at the fastest interval; transfer samples use more. The home screen shows estimated daily traffic. The adjustable 25 MB default cutoff pauses speed samples at the limit; small latency probes continue, so total traffic can exceed it. Lengthen the interval or turn off mobile-data measurement to use less.

THIS IS NOT A SPEED TEST

A speed test answers "how fast is this right now". Honest Signal is a network monitor: it answers "is this working" throughout the day. It does not report dBm — radio strength is the wrong number. PRIVACY

No account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no crash reporting. We run no server, so there is nothing to receive. Measurements, settings and your purchase record stay in the app's private storage, are excluded from cloud backup and device-to-device transfer, and go when you uninstall.
