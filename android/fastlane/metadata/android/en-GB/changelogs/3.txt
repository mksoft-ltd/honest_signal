You asked for a status-bar icon you can actually read.

• It now sits on a solid plate, so it stays legible over any wallpaper and no
  longer looks like your phone's own signal bars. Turn it off in Settings if
  you prefer the old mark.
• Android 16: it also gets an "HS" chip by the clock, clear of the icon row
  that gets cut short when the bar is busy.
• Android 15 and below: the notification carries the score's colour.

Android tints these icons itself, so coloured bars aren't possible.
